01 / PROFILE

About me

I am an Associate Professor (Habilitation to Supervise Research) at the University of Medea, Algeria. My work bridges telecommunications, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and signal processing, with a focus on practical and trustworthy intelligent systems.

My academic activities encompass teaching, graduate supervision, research collaborations, scientific reviewing, and editorial service. Before academia, I worked on core telecommunications networks and SCADA systems.
